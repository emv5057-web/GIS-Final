Why this matters

Housing access is uneven

Median rent varies widely across New York City, shaping who can realistically live in different neighborhoods.

Income changes the map

The affordability calculation uses the common 30% rule: housing is considered affordable when monthly rent is no more than 30% of monthly income.

Manhattan tells a sharper story

The borough-level map introduces the rent geography, while the Manhattan view shows how affordability changes neighborhood by neighborhood.

Methodology

This project uses interactive web mapping to compare estimated median rents with income-based affordability thresholds. A selected income is converted into a monthly affordable rent using the 30% affordability rule.

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Mapbox GL JS, and GeoJSON.
