Why this matters

Housing access is uneven

Median rents vary widely across New York City, shaping who can realistically live in different neighborhoods.

Affordability is a gap, not just yes or no

Instead of only labeling places affordable or unaffordable, this map shows how far estimated rent is above or below an income-based affordable rent.

Neighborhoods tell the story

Clicking each neighborhood reveals how rent, income, and affordability interact at a local scale.

Methodology

This project uses interactive web mapping to compare estimated median rents with income-based affordability thresholds. A selected income is converted into affordable monthly rent using the 30% rule.

The affordability gap is calculated as estimated median rent minus affordable monthly rent. Positive values indicate rent above the selected income threshold.

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Mapbox GL JS, and GeoJSON.
